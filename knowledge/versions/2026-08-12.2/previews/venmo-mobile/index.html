使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Use Venmo Blue ({#008CFF}) as the singular brand color — bright, friendly, not banking-navy。
  • 建议：Render the feed transaction line as mixed weights: actor bold, verb regular, recipient bold。
  • 建议：Use emoji in memos as the primary "what this is for" signal — {🍕🍺 Pizza Friday}。
  • 建议：Keep the Pay / Request split pill pinned at the bottom as the defining CTA。
  • 建议：Render amounts in Venmo Display: 72pt on Pay screen, 56pt on balance, 44pt on confirm。

避免

  • 避免：show amounts on the public feed — Venmo's privacy model hides them except to the two parties。
  • 避免：use navy or dark blue for the brand — Venmo Blue is saturated and bright ({#008CFF})。
  • 避免：separate the dollar sign from the number — it's "$24.50", one unit。
  • 避免：replace emoji in memos with your own icon set — system emoji is the brand。
  • 避免：use pure black for text — {#2F3033} is warmer and softer。
