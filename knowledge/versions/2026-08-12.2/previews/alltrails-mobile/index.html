使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Use a pure-white canvas with a cool sage section surface ({#F2F4F1}) — topo-paper。
  • 建议：Lead with AllTrails Green ({#428000}) for the primary action, active tab, and Record。
  • 建议：Use the difficulty scale as a color language — Easy green / Moderate amber / Hard red — on every trail card and map pin。
  • 建议：Make the trail card the workhorse — photo, difficulty pill, name, location, tabular stat row, rating。
  • 建议：Keep the map full-bleed on Explore with controls floating above it。

避免

  • 避免：use a low-contrast or moody aesthetic — AllTrails must be readable in the field。
  • 避免：repurpose the difficulty colors for decoration — green/amber/red mean difficulty/conditions。
  • 避免：box the map in — on Explore it is full-bleed; UI floats over it。
  • 避免：use thin type weights — legibility outdoors requires 400+ and high contrast。
  • 避免：reflow trail stats with proportional figures — always tabular-nums。
