使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Keep it privacy-minimal: no ads, no algorithmic feed, no vanity counts — calm is the brand。
  • 建议：Use white ({#FFFFFF}) / soft-black ({#1B1B1B}) canvases — never pure {#000000}。
  • 建议：Reserve Signal Blue ({#3A76F0}) for outgoing bubbles, the send button, links, and primary actions。
  • 建议：Make outgoing bubbles blue with white text; incoming neutral gray with primary text。
  • 建议：Show the send circle only when there's text; revert to a mic glyph when empty。

避免

  • 避免：add ads, Stories pressure, streaks, or like-counts — the absence is the feature。
  • 避免：introduce a second accent color — blue is the only one。
  • 避免：put shadows on bubbles — they separate by fill contrast, not elevation。
  • 避免：use pure black canvas in dark mode — {#1B1B1B} is the soft-black target。
  • 避免：show the send button when the composer is empty。
