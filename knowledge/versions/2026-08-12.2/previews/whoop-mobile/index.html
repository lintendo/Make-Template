使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Use {#0A0A0A} as the canvas everywhere — WHOOP is dark-mode-only。
  • 建议：Use Strain Green {#00FF7B} as the primary action color — CTAs, active tabs, links。
  • 建议：Let the Recovery ring interpolate red → yellow → green by score; never override the color。
  • 建议：Use DIN 2014 — license it, or substitute Inter / Barlow when DIN 2014 isn't available。
  • 建议：Set tabular numerals on every metric — HRV, RHR, strain, sleep duration, percentages。

避免

  • 避免：add a light mode — WHOOP is dark-canvas-only by design。
  • 避免：use Strain Green for non-strain metrics — green means strain or "go", recovery has its own ramp。
  • 避免：use neutral drop shadows — the canvas is already too dark; use glows instead。
  • 避免：break the all-caps treatment on headers and buttons — it's the most recognizable typographic move。
  • 避免：compress the Recovery ring smaller than 80pt — below that, the giant number stops being legible。
