使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Use {#FFFFFF} as the canvas with {#FFF2F5} for soft grouped wells; keep one dark anchor (the black hero)。
  • 建议：Use near-black {#0B051D} for structure — headings, amounts, the dark hero。
  • 建议：Reserve Klarna Pink {#FFB3C7} for the primary action and key highlights。
  • 建议：Always put near-black text ({#0B051D}) on Klarna Pink — never white。
  • 建议：Make amounts the heaviest element — 22–36pt weight 700, tabular。

避免

  • 避免：put white text on Klarna Pink — it fails contrast and breaks the brand。
  • 避免：introduce blue, gradients, or a second accent — pink + near-black is the whole story。
  • 避免：use pure black for text — the structural ink is {#0B051D} (violet-cast near-black)。
  • 避免：hide the Pay-in-4 schedule behind a summary — the 4-dot timeline *is* the product。
  • 避免：use proportional figures for money — always tabular, decimal-aligned。
