使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Render the outgoing bubble as a {#0A7CFF → #9D4EDD → #FF5CA0} gradient anchored to the conversation, flowing down the whole run。
  • 建议：Keep incoming bubbles flat neutral gray — energy stays on "you"。
  • 建议：Use white ({#FFFFFF}) light canvas and TRUE black ({#000000}) dark canvas — black makes the gradient glow。
  • 建议：Make the reactions popover bouncy: spring enter, staggered emoji pop, landing bounce。
  • 建议：Show a blue thumbs-up in the empty composer that one-tap sends a 👍 and morphs to send on text。

避免

  • 避免：make outgoing bubbles a flat single color — the conversation-anchored gradient is the brand。
  • 避免：gradient the incoming bubbles — they stay flat gray。
  • 避免：use soft-black in dark mode — Messenger uses pure {#000000} so the gradient luminesces。
  • 避免：make the reactions popover stiff — it must bounce and stagger。
  • 避免：show the filled send button when the composer is empty — it's a thumbs-up then。
