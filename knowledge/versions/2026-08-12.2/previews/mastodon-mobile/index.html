使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Use white {#FFFFFF} light / blue-gray {#191B22} dark — never true black for the dark canvas。
  • 建议：Reserve Mastodon Purple ({#6364FF}) for links, mentions, hashtags, the active tab, FAB, and primary buttons。
  • 建议：Make the boost a 360° spin to {#2DCE89} — it's the signature amplification moment。
  • 建议：Always show the full {@user@instance} handle — identity is federated。
  • 建议：Default content-warning bodies to collapsed with a "SHOW MORE" reveal。

避免

  • 避免：use pure black for dark mode — the blue cast ({#191B22}) is Mastodon's identity。
  • 避免：add accent colors beyond purple and boost-green — the timeline must stay legible。
  • 避免：truncate or hide the instance part of a handle。
  • 避免：auto-expand content warnings — consent-first means collapsed by default。
  • 避免：make avatars circular — Mastodon uses rounded-squares deliberately。
