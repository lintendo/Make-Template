使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Use a bright white canvas ({#FFFFFF}) with the soft neutral surface ({#F2F2F2}) behind cards。
  • 建议：Reserve OkCupid Magenta ({#E2024F}) for the Like, primary CTA, active tab, and match-% badge。
  • 建议：Use indigo ({#0500FF}) for Pass, secondary links, and illustration detail — keep the like/pass duality balanced。
  • 建议：Show a circular match-% badge on every browsable profile, color-tiered by fit。
  • 建议：Animate the match number with a quick count-up the first time a profile appears。

避免

  • 避免：use a thin/industrial substitute font — pick a rounded grotesque (Inter), Larsseit is warm。
  • 避免：add accent colors beyond magenta + indigo to UI chrome — match-tier colors are derived from magenta。
  • 避免：make the match % small or gray when it's high — when it's a great match, it must be loud and magenta。
  • 避免：reduce profiles to thin swipe cards — DoubleTake is rich and internally scrollable。
  • 避免：ship a sterile blank empty state — always use the playful illustration language。
