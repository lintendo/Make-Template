使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Use {#FFFFFF} as light-mode canvas — stark white, not warm-tinted。
  • 建议：Use {#121212} as dark-mode canvas — warm dark, not pure black。
  • 建议：Reserve Pinterest Red ({#E60023}) for the Save button, primary CTAs, and the logomark — that's it。
  • 建议：Render pins at their native aspect ratio in the masonry grid — never force a uniform height。
  • 建议：Use 16pt corner radius on pin images and board covers — softer than most apps, and intentional。

避免

  • 避免：put shadows on pin tiles — the feed looks cleanest flat。
  • 避免：force pins to uniform height — masonry is broken the moment you do。
  • 避免：use red for anything other than the Save button / CTAs / brand — no red text, no red borders, no red accents on metadata。
  • 避免：use tiny corner radii (4pt or less) on images — 16pt is the minimum。
  • 避免：use pure black {#000000} for dark-mode canvas — {#121212} is warmer and reduces eye strain。
