使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Make the post reader the product — a clean white page, serif prose, no boxes。
  • 建议：Set everything-you-read in a reading serif (Source Serif 4 / Spectral); everything-you-operate in Inter。
  • 建议：Body at 18pt with 1.65 line-height and a ~24pt editorial measure — built for long reads。
  • 建议：Make the dek/subtitle italic serif — the editorial-magazine signal。
  • 建议：Ration Substack Orange ({#FF6719}) to the Subscribe CTA, active tab, unread dot, liked heart, pull-quote rule — nothing else。

避免

  • 避免：put cards, borders, or shadows around the reading prose — it flows on bare paper。
  • 避免：use pure black for body ink — warm near-black {#1F1F1F} is correct。
  • 避免：use pure black for dark mode — soft charcoal {#121212}。
  • 避免：set the post body in a UI sans — the article is serif。
  • 避免：add a second accent color — orange + one link blue + neutral greys only。
