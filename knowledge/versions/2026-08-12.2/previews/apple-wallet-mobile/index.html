使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Use {#000000} true black for the canvas — not {#1C1C1E} or system dark mode background — so cards float。
  • 建议：Stack cards with an 80pt peek interval; this is the signature visual。
  • 建议：Keep the card corner radius at exactly 10pt across every card type。
  • 建议：Apply heavy shadows ({rgba(0,0,0,0.5)} at 24pt blur) — cards on black need heavy shadows to lift。
  • 建议：Add a 0.5pt inner top highlight on every card face for that paper-thin glass-edge feel。

避免

  • 避免：use a tab bar — Wallet has none, ever; the stack is the entire navigation。
  • 避免：use dark gray {#1C1C1E} as the canvas; it kills the floating-card effect。
  • 避免：tile cards in a grid — Wallet is always a vertical stack with vanishing-point alignment。
  • 避免：theme card faces from Wallet's side — each issuer ships its own face in the PassKit manifest。
  • 避免：use a smaller card radius than 10pt; don't go above 14pt either — boarding passes use exactly 10pt too。
