使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Use Asana Coral ({#F06A6A}) as the single brand accent — FAB, active tab, primary CTA — identical in light & dark。
  • 建议：Use the charcoal {#1E1F21} for dark canvas — NOT pure black。
  • 建议：Make the completion circle a delight: green fill ({#62D26F}) + checkmark draw + visible feedback state on tap。
  • 建议：Render projects/tags/fields with their assigned hue as a ~16% tint fill + saturated text — never solid slabs。
  • 建议：Use the four-state status system (On track / At risk / Off track / Complete) for project updates。

避免

  • 避免：introduce a second brand accent — coral is the only one; other hues are object colors, not UI accents。
  • 避免：fill pills with solid saturated color — Asana uses ~16% tint + colored text。
  • 避免：use pure black ({#000000}) for the dark canvas or body text。
  • 避免：skip the completion-circle delight — a flat checkbox loses Asana's emotional core。
  • 避免：make status updates look like list rows — they're composed posts with internal padding。
