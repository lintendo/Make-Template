使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Render stickers bubble-less and oversized (~140pt) directly on the chat backdrop, with bounce-in。
  • 建议：Use the periwinkle {#8CABD9} chat backdrop by default (themeable) — not white。
  • 建议：Make outgoing bubbles pale chat-green {#C5F0B1}, incoming white {#FFFFFF}, text black。
  • 建议：Reserve LINE Green {#06C755} for "go" chrome: active tab, primary buttons, official badge, toggles。
  • 建议：Float timestamp + "Read" *outside* the bubble on the backdrop, not inside it。

避免

  • 避免：put stickers in a bubble or cap them at the 70% bubble width — they're bubble-less and big。
  • 避免：use the saturated brand green {#06C755} for the outgoing bubble — that's the pale {#C5F0B1}。
  • 避免：default the chat background to white — the periwinkle backdrop is a brand signature。
  • 避免：put a shadow on stickers — they sit flat on the backdrop; only text bubbles get a faint lift。
  • 避免：bury official-account verification — the green badge is the trust primitive。
