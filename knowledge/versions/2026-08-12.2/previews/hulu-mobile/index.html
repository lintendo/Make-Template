使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Use {#0B0C0F} as the canvas — warm-lifted near-black, NOT pure {#000000} (reserve that for the player)。
  • 建议：Reserve Hulu Green ({#1CE783}) for the "Watch" verb, active tab, and progress — it's the only saturated color。
  • 建议：Use near-black text ({#0B0C0F}) on the green CTA — intentional for contrast on the bright green。
  • 建议：Keep 16:9 landscape tiles at 6pt corner radius — soften, don't round。
  • 建议：Always show the green progress bar on in-progress tiles — resume state is core。

避免

  • 避免：use pure black {#000000} for the app canvas — it crushes thumbnail edges; it's player-only。
  • 避免：add a second accent hue — the single electric green is the entire brand voice。
  • 避免：use white text on the green button — near-black is the intentional contrast choice。
  • 避免：use thin or medium weights — Graphik here is 400 / 600 / 800 only。
  • 避免：round content tiles heavily — 6pt max keeps the 16:9 integrity。
