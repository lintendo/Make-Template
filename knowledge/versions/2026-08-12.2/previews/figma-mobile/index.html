使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Use the five Figma cubes ({#F24E1E}, {#FF7262}, {#A259FF}, {#1ABCFE}, {#0ACF83}) as accent colors on avatars, thumbnails, and cursors — they are the brand。
  • 建议：Use Action Blue {#0D99FF} for primary CTAs and the selection blue in mirror views。
  • 建议：Use Blurple {#5551FF} for Plan upgrade banners, FigJam links, and Slides — *not* for the primary CTA。
  • 建议：Render file cards at 16:10 — Figma's exact aspect。
  • 建议：Use 6pt corner radius on file thumbnails, buttons, input fields — Figma's signature radius。

避免

  • 避免：use a cube color as a primary CTA — they are avatars/cursors/thumbnails only。
  • 避免：use Action Blue {#0D99FF} as the Figma "Blue cube" — they are different blues with different roles。
  • 避免：render the file thumbnail with a non-16:10 aspect。
  • 避免：replace Inter with a humanist sans — Inter's geometric tabular figures are part of the brand。
  • 避免：lowercase hex codes — Figma is uppercase always。
