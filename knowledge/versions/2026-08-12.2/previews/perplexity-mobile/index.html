使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Use the near-black canvas ({#0A0A0A}) as the default — Perplexity's brand is dark-first。
  • 建议：Reserve Perplexity Teal ({#20B8CD}) for the send button, focus rings, citation chip outlines, brand mark, and active states。
  • 建议：Render citation chips inline within the answer prose as {[1]}, {[2]}, {[3]} — never as a footer-only mechanism。
  • 建议：Use FK Grotesk for chrome and brand voice; Inter for answer body。
  • 建议：Use off-white ({#FAFAFA}) for primary text, not pure white — softer on dark canvas。

避免

  • 避免：use a chat-bubble pattern with tails for the question or the answer — Perplexity is search, not chat。
  • 避免：use serif typography for the answer body — Perplexity's voice is encyclopedic, not editorial。
  • 避免：replace the triple-circle mark with a generic search glyph or a stylized "P"。
  • 避免：put citation chips only at the end of the answer — they live inline within the prose。
  • 避免：show the answer without the source card row above it — the citation pattern is the brand。
