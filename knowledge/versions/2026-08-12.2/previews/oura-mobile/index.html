使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Use the cool near-black canvas ({#0B0B0F}) — night with a faint blue cast。
  • 建议：Make the score ring the focal point of every domain screen — large, centered, with air。
  • 建议：Use the tri-domain color language consistently — teal=Readiness, indigo=Sleep, amber=Activity。
  • 建议：Treat color as information — a domain's hue follows it across ring, contributors, trends。
  • 建议：Lead each screen with the number — the score figure is the largest type on screen。

避免

  • 避免：use pure black {#000000} — Oura's charcoal has a faint cool cast。
  • 避免：introduce a fourth accent — only teal, indigo, amber carry domain meaning。
  • 避免：color body text for emphasis — the three hues are reserved for domains/rings。
  • 避免：square the ring's progress cap — it is rounded; the ring is a refined instrument。
  • 避免：drop-shadow the score ring — it is a flat, precise gauge, not a 3D dial。
