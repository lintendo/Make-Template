使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Use {#FFFFFF} as the canvas — Tripadvisor is a bright gallery, not a tinted surface。
  • 建议：Reserve Tripadvisor Green ({#34E0A1}) for the primary action, active tab, and the bubble rating。
  • 建议：Render the five-circle bubble rating as the score unit everywhere — it is the brand signature。
  • 建议：Use black text ({#000000}) on green CTA buttons — the mint is bright; black maximizes contrast。
  • 建议：Lead place cards with full-bleed traveler photography at 3:2, 16pt corner radius。

避免

  • 避免：tint the canvas — keep it pure {#FFFFFF}, not off-white or gray。
  • 避免：use green for body text or as a decorative accent — it is structural only。
  • 避免：draw star ratings — Tripadvisor uses circular bubbles, never stars。
  • 避免：use white text on the green button — black is intentional for contrast。
  • 避免：add a second accent color — the restraint is what makes the green and bubbles iconic。
