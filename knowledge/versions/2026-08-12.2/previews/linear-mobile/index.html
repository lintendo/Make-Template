使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Use {#08090A} as the canvas — near-OLED black, darker than {#121212}。
  • 建议：Reserve Linear Purple ({#5E6AD2}) for primary action, focus, and selection — it is the verb。
  • 建议：Keep issue rows at ~44pt — density is the value proposition。
  • 建议：Use the iconographic status system (drawn glyphs) instead of colored fills or text badges。
  • 建议：Make the command menu (Cmd+K) reachable from anywhere and lightning fast。

避免

  • 避免：use {#121212} — Linear is darker; the near-OLED black is part of the identity。
  • 避免：introduce a second accent color — purple alone carries action and focus。
  • 避免：add a bottom tab bar — navigation is the slide-over sidebar。
  • 避免：pad issue rows for "breathing room" — that destroys the density that makes Linear fast。
  • 避免：use heavy weights — Inter stops at 600 here; no bold titles。
