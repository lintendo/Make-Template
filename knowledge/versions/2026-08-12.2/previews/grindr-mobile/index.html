使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Use pure black {#000000} as the canvas — the photo grid needs a true-black frame。
  • 建议：Reserve Grindr Yellow ({#FFDE00}) for the Tap action, active tab, badges, and the mask logo。
  • 建议：Use black text/icons ({#000000}) on yellow — it is intentional for maximum contrast。
  • 建议：Build the cascade as a 3-column edge-to-edge square grid with a 2pt gutter and 0pt tile radius。
  • 建议：Overlay name + distance on a {rgba(0,0,0,0.65)} bottom scrim so text reads over any photo。

避免

  • 避免：use a softened off-black canvas — Grindr is true {#000000}, the photos demand it。
  • 避免：add accent colors beyond yellow + the green presence dot — restraint makes yellow urgent。
  • 避免：round the cascade tiles — the square, near-touching grid is the brand。
  • 避免：place light or unscrimmed text over a profile photo — always scrim + bold。
  • 避免：show a gray "offline" dot — offline is simply the absence of the green dot。
