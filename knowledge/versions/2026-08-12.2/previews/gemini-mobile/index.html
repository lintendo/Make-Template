使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Treat the conversation as a document — white/charcoal canvas, no cards around turns。
  • 建议：Reserve the Gemini gradient for brand moments: the sparkle, streaming edge, focus ring, chip accents。
  • 建议：Render the user turn as a soft gray chip (trailing-aligned, 4pt pointing corner); the assistant turn as plain text with a leading gradient sparkle。
  • 建议：Use solid Google Blue ({#4285F4}) for the interactive send button and links — the gradient is not a button fill。
  • 建议：Stream answers token-by-token with a gradient shimmer at the leading edge。

避免

  • 避免：bubble the assistant turn — it is plain text on the canvas, not a card。
  • 避免：tint body text with the gradient — text is always solid ink; the gradient is structural。
  • 避免：use the gradient as a flat fill behind paragraphs or as a button background。
  • 避免：add a bottom tab bar — the side drawer is the sole primary navigation。
  • 避免：crowd the prompt bar or remove its generous padding — it is always reachable。
