使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Make the reading page the product — fade ALL chrome out on book open。
  • 建议：Default to Sepia ({#FBF0D9} paper / {#5F4B32} ink) — paper, not screen。
  • 建议：Offer all five reading themes and let the reader's choice override OS appearance。
  • 建议：Set body in a reading serif, justified, hyphenated, 1.72 line-height — it must read like a paperback。
  • 建议：Use Amazon Orange ({#FF9900}) as the ONLY accent — CTA, progress, active-theme ring, selected tab。

避免

  • 避免：put cards, borders, or shadows on the reading page — paper is flat。
  • 避免：tie the reading surface to OS dark mode — the reader's theme choice wins。
  • 避免：use pure white ink on the Black theme — dimmed {#C8C8C8} reduces halation。
  • 避免：introduce a second accent color — orange is the only one; no green/blue CTAs。
  • 避免：set reading body in a UI sans by default — the book is serif。
