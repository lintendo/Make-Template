使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Treat the board as an infinite 2D canvas — pinch-zoom, two-finger pan, absolute object coordinates。
  • 建议：Render a zoom-scaled dotted/square grid as the board reference — {#D7D7DE} light / {#38383F} dark。
  • 建议：Use Miro Yellow ({#FFD02F}) + Miro Ink ({#050038}) for brand/primary actions; glyph on yellow is always ink。
  • 建议：Reserve Miro Blue ({#4262FF}) strictly for selection/interaction state (box, handles, marquee, links)。
  • 建议：Make sticky notes tight-radius (4pt) pastel paper squares with a soft lift shadow + drag-grip。

避免

  • 避免：make the board a scroll view or a fixed-size page — it's an unbounded coordinate plane。
  • 避免：tint the brand with blue or the selection with yellow — yellow = brand/action, blue = selection。
  • 避免：use pure black for text or dim — Miro Ink {#050038} and ink-tinted dim are correct。
  • 避免：give sticky notes large/rounded radii — 4pt tight paper corners are the signature。
  • 避免：dock the toolbar as an opaque bottom bar that frames the canvas — it floats over it。
