使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Use {#FFFFFF} as the canvas; keep the layout bright with one forest anchor (the hero)。
  • 建议：Use forest {#163300} for structure — large titles, the dark hero, primary numbers。
  • 建议：Reserve Bright Green {#9FE870} for the primary action and key highlights。
  • 建议：Always put forest text ({#163300}) on bright green — never white。
  • 建议：Make the total/balance the heaviest element — 32–40pt weight 800, tabular。

避免

  • 避免：put white text on bright green — it fails contrast and breaks the brand。
  • 避免：introduce blue, gradients, or a second accent — forest + bright green is the whole story。
  • 避免：use pure black for text — primary ink is {#0E0F0C}, structure is forest {#163300}。
  • 避免：hide or summarize the fee — the itemized transparency card *is* the product。
  • 避免：use proportional figures for money — always tabular, decimal-aligned。
