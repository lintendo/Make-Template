使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Use pure black {#000000} as the canvas — true black, never warmed or charcoal。
  • 建议：Treat pure white {#FFFFFF} as the only accent — wordmark, one button, active bar, heart outline。
  • 建议：Let the member's photography/video be the ONLY color in the entire experience。
  • 建议：Build the profile as a full-bleed slideshow paced by segmented white story bars。
  • 建议：Score the slideshow with the member's chosen song and show a small white music ticker。

避免

  • 避免：introduce ANY brand color — no like-red, no premium gold, no success green。
  • 避免：warm the black — {#000000} exactly; {#0A0A0A}/{#121212} are too soft for Raya。
  • 避免：add drop shadows — depth is black-on-black with hairlines, never light。
  • 避免：tint, filter, or colored-border the member's media — it is the only color and must stay untouched。
  • 避免：make the profile a static card — it is a music-scored, story-paced slideshow。
