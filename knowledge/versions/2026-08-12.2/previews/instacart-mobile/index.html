使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Use pure white ({#FFFFFF}) as the canvas — the grocery catalog needs a clean shelf。
  • 建议：Reserve Instacart Green ({#0AAD0A}) for actions: Add, stepper, cart bar, checkout, active tab。
  • 建议：Use carrot orange ({#FF7009}) only for deals, savings, and sale prices — it's the incentive color。
  • 建议：Render the quantity stepper as a green pill with white − n + and tabular numerals。
  • 建议：Keep the green cart bar pinned and persistent once items are in the cart。

避免

  • 避免：use a gray or tinted canvas — product photography needs white。
  • 避免：use green for decoration or non-action text — it's the verb color。
  • 避免：use carrot orange for primary buttons — orange is savings, green is action。
  • 避免：omit the persistent cart bar when the cart has items — it's the core loop。
  • 避免：use proportional figures for prices — columns will misalign。
