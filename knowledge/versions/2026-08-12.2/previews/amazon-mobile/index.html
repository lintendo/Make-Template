使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Use Amazon Yellow ({#FF9900}) on every primary purchase CTA — Add to Cart, Buy Now, Search button。
  • 建议：Use black text on yellow buttons ({#0F1111} on {#FF9900}) — the iconic contrast。
  • 建议：Keep the top nav in deep navy {#131921} with a white/yellow search bar。
  • 建议：Pack product tiles with photo + title (2-line clamp) + rating + price + Prime + delivery。
  • 建议：Render prices in Ember 700 at 18-28pt — money is never light。

避免

  • 避免：swap the yellow CTA for another color — Add to Cart is Amazon Yellow, period。
  • 避免：use white text on yellow buttons — intentionally black。
  • 避免：use black canvas — the app is light-first; dark mode is a variant。
  • 避免：over-round buttons — 8pt radius, not pills (the utility feel is the brand)。
  • 避免：hide the search bar — it's the anchor of every screen。
