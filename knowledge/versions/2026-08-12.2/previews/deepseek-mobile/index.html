使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Use a near-black {#0E0E10} canvas (dark) / clean {#FFFFFF} (light) — quiet research-tool calm。
  • 建议：Ration DeepSeek Blue {#4D6BFE} to the whale, user bubble, send, active toggles, links, citations。
  • 建议：Render the reasoning trace in a recessed {#16171A} panel with a 2pt blue left-bar。
  • 建议：Keep the chain-of-thought dimmed italic {#8A8B90} — italic IS the "internal monologue" signal。
  • 建议：Keep the answer upright and full-contrast {#ECECEC} — visually dominant over the trace。

避免

  • 避免：render the reasoning trace upright or full-contrast — it must be dimmer/italic/recessed。
  • 避免：make the trace background lighter than the canvas — it recedes by being *darker*。
  • 避免：use blue for body text — blue is reserved for accents/toggles/links/citations only。
  • 避免：give assistant messages a bubble — only the user gets a bubble; AI is full-width。
  • 避免：add a second accent color — DeepSeek has exactly one ({#4D6BFE})。
