使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Use pure white ({#FFFFFF}) as the light canvas; warm near-black {#121212} for dark — never true black。
  • 建议：Use Deliveroo Teal ({#00CCBC}) as the ONE brand color — logo, buttons, ratings, Plus, active tab, basket, floating {+}。
  • 建议：Put {#003733} (Teal Ink) — not white — as the content color on teal fills。
  • 建议：Lead every restaurant card with a wide 5:3 photo in a 14pt rounded container。
  • 建议：Show the fee pill (teal moped icon + "£X delivery · Min £Y") on every restaurant card。

避免

  • 避免：introduce a second brand accent — Deliveroo is intentionally mono-teal。
  • 避免：put white text on a teal button — use Teal Ink {#003733}。
  • 避免：crop restaurant photos to squares or tint them — wide 5:3 and full-color。
  • 避免：drop the fee pill — the delivery fee + minimum chip is a Deliveroo card signature。
  • 避免：replace the floating {+} with a full-width "Add" button on menu rows — the corner {+} is the gesture。
