使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Use pure black {#000000} as the canvas — Peloton is a dark studio, always。
  • 建议：Keep Peloton Red {#DF1E2E} as the *only* brand color; brighten to {#FF4B57} for text/active on black。
  • 建议：Treat in-class metric colors (Cadence {#E5402A}, Resistance {#F0A030}, Strive/HR {#3DB8E0}) as fixed semantics — same hue every time。
  • 建议：Make class thumbnails cinematic and full-bleed with a baked gradient scrim for text legibility。
  • 建议：Pulse the white dot in the red LIVE badge — it's the brand heartbeat。

避免

  • 避免：use charcoal ({#121212} is a card, not the canvas) — the base is *true* black {#000000}。
  • 避免：introduce a second brand color — there is only Peloton Red。
  • 避免：recolor in-class metrics per theme — their color *is* the data。
  • 避免：ship a light mode — Peloton is studio-black by design。
  • 避免：set titles light or wide — Peloton type is 800–900 with negative tracking。
