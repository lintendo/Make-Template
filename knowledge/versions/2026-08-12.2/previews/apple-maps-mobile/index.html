使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Use the cream-paper canvas {#F6F1E6} for the map land surface — this is the most recognizable Apple Maps visual。
  • 建议：Use Maps Blue {#0A84FF} for the directions polyline, current-location puck, and primary CTAs。
  • 建议：Use Maps Red {#FF3B30} (the iOS system red) for the dropped pin and place-card identity。
  • 建议：Use {translucent backdrop blur} blur on the search card, map controls, step card, ETA bar — blur IS the depth system。
  • 建议：Use SF Pro at the system stack (Display, Text, Compact) — no custom face。

避免

  • 避免：use a custom map style that overrides the cream {#F6F1E6} land color — it's signature。
  • 避免：use a solid background on the search card — {translucent backdrop blur} blur is non-negotiable。
  • 避免：put the current-location button at the bottom — it stacks in the top-right with the layer and 3D buttons。
  • 避免：replace SF Pro with a custom font — Apple Maps is intentionally system-native。
  • 避免：omit tabular figures from times and distances — column alignment matters。
