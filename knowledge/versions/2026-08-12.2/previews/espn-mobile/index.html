使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Use near-black ({#0E0F11}) as the canvas — NOT pure black; cards and the ticker must lift off it。
  • 建议：Pin a horizontal scores ticker near the top — it's the fastest path to "what's the score"。
  • 建议：Use ESPN Red ({#D50A0A} / bright {#CC0000}) as the only brand accent — logo, CTA, active-tab dot, tags。
  • 建议：Use a hotter red ({#FF1A1A}) + a pulsing dot for LIVE games; dim finals to gray。
  • 建议：Turn the winning team's score green ({#1FAA59}) on finished games — a result readable at a glance。

避免

  • 避免：use pure black ({#000000}) — ESPN's ground is near-black so cards have contrast。
  • 避免：introduce a second brand accent — red is it; live red and win green are *status*, not accents。
  • 避免：use proportional numerals for scores — digits will shift and the scoreboard breaks。
  • 避免：hide or bury the scores ticker — it is the product's spine。
  • 避免：use a tint pill for the active tab — ESPN uses a red dot under a white icon。
