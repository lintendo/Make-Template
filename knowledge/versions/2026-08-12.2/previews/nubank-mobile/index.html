使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Make Nu Purple ({#820AD1}) the identity — a full-bleed purple hero header is the first thing the user sees, identical in light and dark。
  • 建议：Build the Home as a calm stack of tiles — NuConta balance, quick actions, credit invoice, activity。
  • 建议：Follow "fewer, clearer choices" — surface the common actions large; push everything else one tap deeper。
  • 建议：Render the Roxinho as a clean purple gradient card — the brand's most recognized object。
  • 建议：Use big confident currency numbers with tabular figures for balances and invoice totals。

避免

  • 避免：dilute the purple — no rainbow of brand colors; Nu Purple owns the identity。
  • 避免：use pure black for dark mode — deep aubergine {#15101C} is correct。
  • 避免：use pure white for the light canvas — faint lilac-white {#F4F2F7}, white only for tiles。
  • 避免：cram the Home with dense dashboards, charts, or stacked banners — it is intentionally short。
  • 避免：put a shadow on the purple hero — it is a flat color block。
