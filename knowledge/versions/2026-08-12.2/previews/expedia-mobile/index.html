使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Use Expedia Yellow ({#FFC94D}) strictly for savings/deal/loyalty highlights — bundle tags, member prices, One Key chip, the "Bundle & save" CTA。
  • 建议：Put navy {#00355F} text on yellow, white on Action Blue {#1668E3}。
  • 建议：Lead every result card with a 16:10 photo; deal flag top-left, save heart top-right。
  • 建议：Show the review-score badge with score + word + count ("9.2 Wonderful · 1,847 reviews")。
  • 建议：Use the strikethrough → discounted price pattern when there is a real saving。

避免

  • 避免：use Expedia Yellow for generic chrome, body text, or non-savings UI — it dilutes the savings signal。
  • 避免：put white text on yellow or yellow text on blue (contrast + brand violation)。
  • 避免：use One Key gold for anything other than rewards (no generic icons, no body accents)。
  • 避免：show a strikethrough price when there is no real discount。
  • 避免：bury the review-score badge — it's the primary trust signal and must front the card。
