使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Use the cream canvas ({#FFF5E1}) — the kraft-paper warmth is core to the brand。
  • 建议：Reserve Chipotle Red ({#A81612}) for action: Add to Bag, order CTA, rewards, selected ingredients, active tab。
  • 建议：Set headers in ALL-CAPS bold condensed Archivo with positive tracking — the menu-board voice。
  • 建议：Keep body and ingredient names calm sentence-case Avenir — the quiet counterpoint。
  • 建议：Use espresso brown ({#451400}) for text — never pure black。

避免

  • 避免：use stark white as the canvas — the cream warmth is the brand。
  • 避免：use red for decoration or non-action text — it's the action color。
  • 避免：set body copy in ALL CAPS — only headers and buttons shout。
  • 避免：use pure black for any text — brown carries the food-forward palette。
  • 避免：use fully-rounded pill buttons — Chipotle's buttons are squared 8pt。
