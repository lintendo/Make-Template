使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Use pure true-black {#000000} as the canvas — OLED pixels off, art floats。
  • 建议：Inset the hero card (14pt) with a 16pt corner radius — the signature floating-gallery treatment。
  • 建议：Use SF Pro (or {-apple-system}) and follow HIG text styles — Large Title / Body / Caption。
  • 建议：Keep system blue {#0A84FF} as the *only* accent — active tab + text links, used as the iOS link role。
  • 建议：Make "Play" a solid white {#FFFFFF} button with a black label, 12pt rounded。

避免

  • 避免：use a charcoal canvas — it must be pure {#000000} (the floating-art effect depends on it)。
  • 避免：make the hero full-bleed — it is inset and rounded; full-bleed breaks the gallery identity。
  • 避免：introduce a second accent or any gradient — blue is the only accent, MLS pink is the only exception。
  • 避免：tint the Play button — it is always white with a black label。
  • 避免：add badges/flags to thumbnails beyond the channel tag and the rare LIVE/MLS chip。
