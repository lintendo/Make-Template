使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Use the cream canvas ({#FDF8F2}) everywhere — it's the brand's most recognizable surface treatment。
  • 建议：Reserve Rose Gold ({#E8A04D}) for Standouts, Roses, and premium edges — nothing else。
  • 建议：Render the "H" mark in Hinge Black ({#1A1A1A}) on cream, or Paper White on Hinge Black — never gold, never any other color except the launch-screen variant。
  • 建议：Use Sailec for display, prompts, names, and CTAs; Inter or system for attribute chips and chrome。
  • 建议：Make every prompt card and photo independently tappable — the unit of interaction is "a moment on the profile", not "a profile"。

避免

  • 避免：use a Tinder-style swipe stack — Hinge profiles are vertical scrolls, not card decks. The interaction model IS the design。
  • 避免：render the canvas in pure white ({#FFFFFF}) — cream ({#FDF8F2}) is the signature。
  • 避免：use gold ({#E8A04D}) anywhere except Standouts, Roses, and the Rose CTA — it loses its meaning if it appears in chrome。
  • 避免：use pure black ({#000000}) for type — {#1A1A1A} is warm and intentional。
  • 避免：make the prompt question larger than the answer — the user's words should always be the loudest element。
